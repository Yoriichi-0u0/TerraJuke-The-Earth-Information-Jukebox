## What changed for visitors

- About answers "why a jukebox?" in three cards: pick a record, hear it, check it.
- It lists the eight records with their sources and a link to hear each one.
- It explains the two ways to listen (the picture, the numbers) in plain words.
- "Made for everyone" lists captions, keyboard play, reduced motion and themes, and says what has not been tested.
- "How we used AI" matches the portal's SI section: code, watermarked artwork and the demo's narration voice; no
  data or in-app sound.
- Team BoboMimi, with links to the demo video and the source code. Science and sources stay at `#sources`.

With this, all five pages (Home, Listen, Create, Explore Data, About) share the all-ages style.
