## What changed for visitors

- **Record 07 The Warming Map** replaces *Temperature Through Time*. NASA SVS 5450, *Global Temperature Anomalies
  from 1880 to 2024* (GISTEMP v4), one map per decade. Side A plays 34 bands pole to pole for any decade; Side B plays
  any place's 15 decades. Colours are read back to °C on NASA's −2 to +2 °C colour bar.
- **Aurora Broadcast**: a new visual identity on every page (aurora sky, marquee lights, a jukebox-arch player that
  breathes while it plays, glowing transport, a cream Daylight Radio light mode).
- Mystery Record draws from Climate Spiral and Moving Earth (its measurement round is gone with record 07).

## NASA sources touched

- **Added:** NASA SVS 5450 (15 Science On a Sphere frames, NASA's colour bar and `time.txt`), pinned by SHA-256 from
  Internet Archive raw captures. Global means read from colour agree with NASA's GISTEMP within 0.06 °C on average.
  [WARMING-MAP.md](../WARMING-MAP.md).
- **Removed from the shelf:** the GISTEMP replay record (the numbers remain as Climate Spiral's Side B).
