## What changed for visitors

- Explore Data starts with the same two big data buttons as Create (Earth's temperature, Arctic sea ice) and three
  steps: pick a month, read the number, hear its note.
- The number is said in a sentence: "January 2025 was 1.37 °C warmer than an average January in 1951–1980."
- New section **All eight records, and what their numbers are**: each record is labelled as NASA measurements, read
  from NASA's colour scale, counted from NASA's map, or picture brightness only. Climate Spiral, The Ice Year, The
  Warming Map and Storm Drums show a small chart, a takeaway computed from the same committed data the records play,
  and a CSV download. Every card links to hear the record and to its source.

No new data or sound rules.
