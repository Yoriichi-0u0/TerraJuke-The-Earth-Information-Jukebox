## What changed for visitors

- **Learn** (`/learn`, in the menu between Explore Data and About): one card per record with **Listen for** (what to
  play and notice, with a link to hear it) and a three-choice question. Picking an answer says "Yes!" or "Not
  quite." and why, using the record's own sound rule. Examples: The Ice Year, the lowest note is in September
  because less sea ice plays lower; The Warming Map, the Arctic rings highest in 2024; Moving Earth, a brighter frame
  is a brighter picture, not a hotter Earth. A running score and Start again; nothing is saved or sent.
- On phones held sideways, Play on Listen is back on the first screen (the three-step strip gives way on short
  landscape screens).

## NASA sources touched

None. Every answer restates a fixed mapping rule or a fact the record's tests already check.
