## What changed for visitors

- Create opens with three steps: pick the data, pick a sound, play and save.
- Two big choices for the data replace the "Earth Station" menu: Earth's temperature (NASA GISTEMP, one year) or
  Arctic sea ice (NSIDC Sea Ice Index, twelve Septembers).
- A plain box says what you change (instrument, style, speed, echo) and what the data decides (which note plays,
  on a fixed scale); drums, harmony and echo never change a measurement.
- A new card leads to Your Record: Listen scrolls the photo panel into view with Choose image ready.

No change to sound rules, data or audio code.
