## What changed for visitors

- On Explore Data, the Climate Spiral and The Warming Map charts colour warmer-than-normal bars orange and cooler
  bars blue (the usual convention, and the direction of NASA's own maps) instead of teal and pink. The Ice Year and
  Storm Drums charts keep one accent colour. Colours have at least 3:1 graphic contrast in light and dark.
