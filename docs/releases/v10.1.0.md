## What changed for visitors

- Listen starts with three steps (pick a record, press Play, flip to Side B) and one line on why it is a jukebox.
- The first record says START HERE; record subtitles, side names and captions use everyday words.
- Advanced sound choices sit under "More sound options"; small text is larger.
- Phones with 2 GB of memory or less skip the decorative 3D shelf.
- Home explains the idea in plain language.
