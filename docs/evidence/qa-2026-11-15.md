## Findings

1. **The Ice Year test raced the image download** (Chromium, production). The test read NASA's frame before it had
   finished loading over the network. Test fix: wait for the image (up to 15 s). Not a product bug.
2. **Real bug: on phones, The Warming Map's and Storm Drums' NASA map collapsed to zero height.** The paired artwork
   gives the panel a fixed height on phones and the map, a flex item, shrank to nothing, so Side B (touch a place) was
   impossible on phones. Fixed in `WarmingDeck.css` (the panel keeps its natural height; the map does not shrink). The
   Ice Year already had its own override. Tests now also check that each map keeps its 2:1 shape on every screen.
3. **Storm Drums Side B test tapped 2° from a place boundary**; on a 320 px map one pixel is about 1°, so the tap could
   land in the neighbouring place. Test fix: tap the centre of the place.
4. One Android-tablet run of a Climate Spiral decode-failure test failed once (passed on retry, reported flaky); a
   pre-existing timing-sensitive test, not touched today.
